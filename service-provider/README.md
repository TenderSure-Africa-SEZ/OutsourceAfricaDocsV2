# Service Provider Guide

As a Service Provider, you use Trific to get vetted for the service categories you work in, find and apply to jobs, deliver work against contracts and milestones, and get paid.

Start here:

1. **[Profile & Portfolio](profile-and-portfolio.md)** — setting up your company profile, rates, and past-work samples.
2. **[Vetting & Onboarding Status](vetting-and-onboarding-status.md)** — how to submit for vetting before you can apply to jobs (review/approval happens outside this portal — see the note below).
3. **[Finding & Applying to Jobs](finding-and-applying-to-jobs.md)** — browsing open jobs and submitting a proposal.
4. **[Contracts & Milestones](contracts-and-milestones.md)** — accepting a contract and submitting milestone reports.
5. **[Payments & Wallet](payments-and-wallet.md)** — tracking what you've earned and what's in escrow.
6. **[Users & Roles](users-and-roles.md)** — adding colleagues to your company account.
7. **[Messaging & Clients](messaging-and-clients.md)** — talking to clients and reviewing your work history with them.

> **A note on vetting:** this portal is only where you *submit* your compliance documents and vetting payment. The actual review and approval is carried out by Trific's partner, TenderSure, on a separate internal system — you won't see that review queue here, only your own status.
