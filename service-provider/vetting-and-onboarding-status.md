# Vetting & Onboarding Status

Before a service provider company can apply to jobs, it must be **vetted**: confirming who you are and which service categories you're qualified to work in.

Vetting and onboarding happen entirely on **TenderSure**, not in this Service Provider portal. This portal's own vetting and prequalification pages (`/service-provider/vetting` and `/service-provider/prequalification`) still exist in the app today, but they are being phased out and should not be used or documented as the active flow.

If you need to get a company vetted, do it through TenderSure rather than through this portal.
