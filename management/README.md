# Management Guide

As a member of Trific's Management team, you're the platform's gatekeeper and back office: you approve the projects and contracts that make work billable, oversee every Client and Service Provider account, and manage commission and payments for both Trific and its partner TenderSure.

Start here:

1. **[Dashboard](dashboard.md)** — the platform-wide numbers you see when you log in.
2. **[Reviewing & Approving Projects and Contracts](reviewing-projects-and-contracts.md)** — the core gatekeeping workflow, walked through end to end on a real example.
3. **[Managing Clients & Providers](managing-clients-and-providers.md)** — account oversight for both sides of the marketplace.
4. **[Jobs & Create Job](jobs-and-create-job.md)** — turning an approved project into a job a service provider can be matched to.
5. **[Payments & Commissions](payments-and-commissions.md)** — service provider payouts, and commission for both Trific and TenderSure.
6. **[User Statistics](user-statistics.md)** — platform growth and user-type reporting.
7. **[Document Archive](document-archive.md)** — the read-only records store, organized by category and service provider.
8. **[Users & Roles](users-and-roles.md)** — administering Management's own staff accounts and privileges.
9. **[Settings](settings.md)** — tags, commission rates, and bank accounts.

> **Out of scope:** Service provider vetting (reviewing compliance documents and approving a provider for a category) is not a Management function — it's owned entirely by the separate TenderSure Admin portal.
