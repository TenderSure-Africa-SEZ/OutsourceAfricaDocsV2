# Client Guide

As a Client, you use Global Business Gateway to post work, find vetted service providers, agree contracts, and pay for delivered services, all in one place.

Start here:

1. **[Dashboard](dashboard.md)**: what you see when you log in.
2. **[Creating a Project](creating-a-project.md)**: how every piece of work begins.
3. **[Finding Service Providers](finding-service-providers.md)**: browsing and inviting vetted providers.
4. **[Contracts & Milestones](contracts-and-milestones.md)**: reviewing terms, tracking and approving milestones.
5. **[Payments & Wallet](payments-and-wallet.md)**: funding a contract deposit and viewing statements.
6. **[Users & Roles](users-and-roles.md)**: adding colleagues to your company account and controlling what they can do.
7. **[Profile & Compliance](profile-and-compliance.md)**: your personal profile and company KYC.
8. **[Messaging, Reviews & Archive](messaging-reviews-archive.md)**: talking to providers and rating completed work.

The single most important thing to understand as a Client is the approval pipeline your projects go through: see the [platform overview](../README.md#how-the-pieces-fit-together) for the full picture.
